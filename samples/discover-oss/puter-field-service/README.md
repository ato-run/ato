# Puter Field Service staging observation

On 2026-11-06, the [Puter Field Service Early Alpha listing](https://stg-app.ato.run/?c=dapp_01M6N4WZGCMCG8MR9Y4247370J)
opened its public trial from the window's `…` menu in a separate Chrome tab.
In the built-in sample business, marking a sample visit complete changed the
completed-appointment count and added that job to the invoicing-needed list.
The demo states that changes stay in that tab and disappear when it is left.

The embedded frame was still loading when the new-tab action was selected;
a completed embedded launch, signed-in persistence, sharing and actual
business workflows remain unverified. The app warns against relying on its
early-alpha state for real business records. This Puter application is not
Beveren FSM and does not satisfy Beveren's staging acceptance. The exact
source revision and complete distribution provenance of the listing remain
unverified.
