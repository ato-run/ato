# Puter Legal staging observation

On 2026-11-06, the [Puter Legal Early Alpha listing](https://stg-app.ato.run/?c=dapp_01M6N51JCXGCK7AAGV1ZD166MN)
opened its public trial from the window's `…` menu in a separate Chrome tab.
In the built-in sample firm, completing a sample task reduced the outstanding
count from 37 to 36 and increased the completed count from three to four.
The demo states that changes stay in that tab and disappear when it is left.

The embedded frame was still loading when the new-tab action was selected;
a completed embedded launch and signed-in persistence remain unverified. The
app warns against using this early-alpha version for real client matters,
trust funds or billing. This is Puter Legal, not FreeEed. The exact source
revision and complete distribution provenance for this staging listing remain
unverified.
